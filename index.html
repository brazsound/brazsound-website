About me

MixingRecordingMusic editing

I’m Matheus Braz. Born in Brazil, based in Los Angeles, and always listening.

I work closely with artists and producers to help records feel intentional, honest, and fully realized. My approach is collaborative and instinct-driven, focused on emotion, balance, and musicality.

Whether I’m involved early in the process or coming in at the final stages, my role is to support the vision, elevate the music, and preserve what makes a song feel alive.
